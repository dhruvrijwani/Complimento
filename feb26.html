04   Platform Analysis

Meta & Google — What Happened

A detailed account of campaign behaviour, optimisations, and learnings across both platforms in February.

M

Meta

In the initial week, we observed strong performance; however, it gradually started declining from 15th February onwards.

Two of our campaigns suddenly experienced a drop in performance mid-month. Despite multiple optimisations and testing new ads, they were unable to regain their previous momentum.

As part of the optimisation process, we paused one of the long-performing campaigns and consolidated the structure into two campaigns focused only on Sehr and Nita Ambani collections, while introducing newer creatives to regain momentum.

Along with this consolidation, we began testing new content strategy creatives. We initially saw traction with the celebrity-led video and the "Hum Fraud Hain" founder-led video. These are now being moved into BAU for March, while new creatives will be introduced for further testing.

Since we began testing new content strategies in the second half of the month, we were unable to fully utilise the testing budget and ended up spending only ₹30K on testing.

Toward the end of February and early March, we have started seeing a gradual uptick in performance for both Sehr and Nita Ambani collections.

The major drop in the funnel was driven by an increase in CPC and a decline in CTR, which we have now identified and are actively working to improve.

ROAS was also impacted due to a slight drop in AOV, primarily because the number of Nita Ambani necklaces sold decreased, bringing the overall average down.

On the positive side, we observed upward traction for two SKUs — the Ruhi Stunning Gold Chain Bracelet and the 3-Layered Ruhi Necklace.

We also noticed that new creatives redirecting to the Sehr collection page were leading customers to purchase lower AOV products, so we shifted the destination back to the Sehr 7-layer product page to maintain stronger AOV performance.

G

Google

On Google, we are continuing with a very small budget of ₹300 per day, primarily to protect brand searches from users who look us up on Google after seeing our ads on Meta.

Notes for March

Strategic Priorities & Action Items

Focus more on unusual content strategies (as discussed in February) that create strong recall and remembrance in customers' minds.

Work towards developing more repeat buyers.

Continue pushing marketing messages through Engage360 during festive or special occasions with different messaging angles and product highlights.

If possible, start manually reviewing abandoned carts to identify recovery opportunities.

Founders should become more camera- and content-friendly to support storytelling and brand connection.

Begin sharing more founder-led and UGC-led content, with a minimum of two pieces from each category every week, ensuring timelines are not missed.

Increase the production of UGC content to strengthen social proof.

New summer collection products — Kruti Arts and Raas Leela — will be rigorously tested this month.

Additionally, we will test Click-to-WhatsApp campaigns to evaluate whether we can generate a meaningful number of orders through this channel.
