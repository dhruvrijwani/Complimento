Executive Summary

January delivered solid performance with ₹7.01L in revenue across 158 orders, achieving a 1.64x ROAS. Meta campaigns dominated as the primary revenue driver with 100% of orders. BAU campaigns excelled with 2.02x ROAS, while Testing campaigns at 1.14x ROAS present opportunities for optimization.

In January, we exceeded the revenue target by achieving 103.26%, generating ₹7,01,462 in revenue. However, this was accompanied by a 15% overspend, and ROAS fell short of the target by 10%, closing at 1.64x.

📈 Performance Evolution & Strategic Pivots

Initial Performance Trend: The initial week saw good performance, but it gradually slowed down as the month progressed, requiring strategic interventions.

Product Performance & Optimization Journey

👗
Neeta Ambani Necklace - Revival Story
  • Challenge: One of our top-performing winner products had been consistently declining in performance by the end of December, recording 0 orders till around 70% of the month
  • Strategy: Paused non-performing campaigns and started testing with past high-performing audiences using new creatives alongside proven winner creatives
  • Result: Successfully revived the product starting with slower traction, then gradually built up good performance with better CAC and ROAS
🔍
New Product Testing Challenges
  • Parallel Testing: Along with Nita Ambani testing, new product finding testing was live but couldn't identify any winners
  • Kundan Green Pearl Choker: Best selling among new products but consistently failed to meet benchmark metrics for CAC and ROAS, leading to pause
  • Outcome: After multiple optimizations with no products gaining good traction, testing campaigns were paused to focus resources on proven winners
🎯
Strategic Consolidation Decision

By the initial part of last week, we decided to consolidate all testings and avoid thin budget splitting across different campaigns. Focus shifted to reviving the Nita Ambani necklace winner product to ensure we don't rely on just 1 winner product (Sehr Ruhi 7 Layer Necklace) and to avoid huge inventory blockage for Nita Ambani necklace.

Operational Challenges & Solutions

⚠️
Payment Processing Issue

Challenge: 1.5 days straight pause in ads due to unavailability of funds. Auto-payment got stuck in "processing" stage and a few auto-debit transactions failed, resulting in:

  • Almost 1.5 day revenue loss
  • Next day higher ad spend needed to cover the lost spend
  • Negative impact on ROAS

Solution Implemented: Decided to be cautious by maintaining available funds in the ad account always and refilling before it ends to avoid auto-debit failures

Creative Performance Analysis

Understanding what resonated with audiences and what needed optimization

✅

What Worked

  • •
    Add-to-Cart CTA with Anchor Pricing

    Retargeting campaign featuring add-to-cart CTA with anchor pricing visuals worked exceptionally well for Sehr Choker piece, driving conversions effectively

  • •
    Simple Bold Offer Format

    Straight, simple bold offer with just the product gained good traction overall. However, none of them has achieved winner status yet

  • •
    Voiceover Creative Innovation

    The new voiceover format for Nita Ambani product picked up nicely and is starting to perform well, showing strong early performance indicators

❌

What Didn't Work

  • •
    Nita Ambani Winner Creative Decline

    Past very well-performing winner creatives of Nita Ambani necklace started declining to nearly 0 results, requiring complete creative refresh

  • •
    Sehr Necklace Creative Fatigue

    Winner creative of Sehr necklace also started to slow down, but gradually picked up after adding competitive newer creatives with various angles

  • •
    Sehr Choker Performance Drop

    Sehr Choker necklace performing creative declined in performance, leading to higher CAC and lower ROAS, necessitating creative optimization

💡 Key Takeaway: Creative Fatigue Management

The analysis reveals a critical pattern: even winning creatives experience performance decay over time. Success requires continuous creative testing, diversification, and refresh cycles. The recovery of Sehr necklace through new angle variations and the promising performance of voiceover formats demonstrate that systematic creative innovation is essential for maintaining campaign performance.
