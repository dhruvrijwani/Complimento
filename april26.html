April marks the highest revenue month at ₹7.58L and the best ROAS at 1.97x in the last four months, continuing the upward trend from the February dip. The February dip (₹5.92L revenue) served as an inflection point, after which both revenue and efficiency have trended upward.

Meta Platform

Meta Platform Deep Dive

Detailed analysis of campaign performance, challenges, and optimization efforts

📉 Performance Context
In April, we fell short of the revenue target by ~5%, with ROAS also coming in ~1.5% below target. This performance was alongside a 4% underspend, primarily due to no product testing being executed as planned. A ~24% drop in checkout initiated rate compared to March was observed, though an improved COI-to-order rate along with higher AOV helped balance the overall results.
💰
AOV Growth - 11% MoM Increase
Average Order Value increased 11% month-on-month to ₹4,620, primarily driven by a higher contribution from combo product sales and increased sales of the Nita Ambani necklaces. This AOV uplift was a critical factor in maintaining revenue levels despite the drop in checkout initiation volume.
🚫
Product Testing - Not Executed
No product testing plan could be executed during April. This was a missed opportunity to identify new winner products and diversify the revenue base beyond the current top performers.

💎 Product Performance Insight
The top 2 products alone account for 75.1% of total revenue (₹5.7L out of ₹7.58L), with the Ruhi 7-Layer Gold Necklace at ₹3.4L (44.8%) and Nita Ambani CZ Diamond Necklace at ₹2.3L (30.3%). This heavy concentration on just two hero products underscores the critical need for product testing that was missed in April. Without new winner products being identified, the brand remains highly vulnerable to creative fatigue or demand shifts in these two SKUs. The remaining catalog (products 3-5 + others) contributes only ₹1.58L (24.9%), indicating significant untapped potential for product diversification.
⚠️ Revenue Concentration Risk
With no product testing executed in April due to content delays and the parallel ad account issue, the revenue base remains dangerously concentrated. Restarting product testing in May is essential to identify new winners, reduce dependency on the Ruhi 7-Layer and Nita Ambani necklaces, and build a more resilient revenue portfolio.
Creative Insights

Creative Performance Analysis

Understanding what resonated with audiences and key challenges this month

✅ What Worked

  • Higher AOV Strategy - Combo product sales and Nita Ambani necklace focus drove AOV up 11% MoM to ₹4,620, compensating for lower checkout volumes
  • Improved COI-to-Order Rate - Despite a 24% drop in checkout initiated rate, the conversion from checkout to order improved, helping balance overall results
  • Google YouTube Placements - 71% of Google orders came through YouTube, validating video-first product discovery for jewellery
  • Stable Cancellation Rate - 7.20% cancellation rate held steady, maintaining operational efficiency gains from prior months

❌ What Didn't Work

  • Parallel Ad Account Conflict - In-house team running same creatives from separate account caused auction overlap, attribution issues, and creative performance decline
  • No Product Testing Executed - Missed opportunity to diversify the product portfolio and find new winner products on Meta
  • Checkout Initiated Rate Drop - 24% decline in checkout initiated rate vs March signals potential audience fatigue or funnel friction
  • Revenue Target Miss - Fell short by ~5% of the revenue target, with ROAS ~1.5% below target

Looking Ahead

Strategic Notes & Action Items for May

Key focus areas and recommendations based on April's performance

📢 Content Sharing Request
In April, only 1 founder-led and 1 UGC content piece were provided, that too towards the end of the month. We request a more aggressive and consistent approach towards content sharing so that seamless content testing can continue. Product testing could not be executed in April due to the lack of content and testing products. Now that we've received most of the required assets, we'll begin product testing accordingly. However, we request more prompt sharing of content, as delays reduce the time available for execution and can further impact delivery timelines.
