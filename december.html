In December, we had estimated a spend of ₹373K. To ensure better ROI, we optimized for ROAS and ultimately spent ₹381K (102% of the projected budget), achieving a ROAS of 1.55 and Revenue of ₹5,90,402.

In December, the Ruhi 7 Layer Necklace remained our best seller, while the Nita Ambani necklace ranked as the second best seller, though with lower sales compared to the previous month. The Sehr Choker Gold Brass Necklace held the third position. Additionally, within the Kundan collection, the Kundan Green Pearl Choker Necklace Set showed good performance.

Note: In December, our primary focus was on maintaining our ROAS target as the top priority while also scaling the spends to reach the revenue target.

Excluding November Cancellations: In the month of December, cancellations worth ₹34,927 were recorded for orders that belonged to November. If we exclude these cancellations from December's data, the effective ROAS for December improves to 1.64 with revenue of ₹6,25,329.
