In December, we anticipated higher scale to drive revenue growth and estimated a spend of ₹373K. While prioritizing ROAS stability, we ultimately spent ₹381K (102% of projected) and achieved a ROAS of 1.55, generating a total revenue of ₹5,90,402.

The Ruhi 7-Layer Necklace continued to be our top-performing product, followed by the Nita Ambani Necklace as the second best seller, although with relatively lower sales compared to December. The Sehr Choker Gold Brass Necklace secured the third position, while within the Kundan collection, the Kundan Green Pearl Choker Necklace Set showed promising performance.

Our primary focus throughout December remained on maintaining the ROAS benchmark as the top priority, while simultaneously scaling spends to ensure we stayed on track to achieve the overall revenue target.

Additionally, November included cancellations worth ₹34,927 that were attributed to December orders. Excluding these cancellations from December’s performance, the effective revenue stands at ₹6,25,329, improving the effective ROAS to 1.64.

Key Insights & Recommendations

  • We observed a dip in both sales and ROAS this month; however, performance remained close to our projected estimates.
  • The Ruhi 7 Layer Necklace continued to be our best seller, followed by the Nita Ambani Necklace in second position and the Sehr Gold Brass Choker as the third best seller.
  • From the Kundan collection, the Kundan Green Pearl Necklace showed strong performance.
  • The Wedding campaign did not perform as expected and was paused. In its place, the creative testing campaign was repositioned into a Sehr Collection–focused campaign to test Sehr-specific products more effectively.
  • The previously best-performing campaign, CBO_3Aud_Broad_19Jul25, showed a consistent decline over the last 15 days of December despite multiple optimizations.
  • A new LAL audience using top-performing creatives-which had achieved an overall ROAS of 2+ in November-also performed well in December, continuing to reach and engage relevant audience segments effectively.
  • A new product testing campaign was initiated with two ad sets-one featuring new products with new shoot creatives, and another featuring previously performing products with new shoot creatives-which delivered encouraging results.
  • In this campaign, most of the spend was driven by Meta toward performing products with new creatives. Among these, the Ruhi 7 Layer Necklace received the highest spend and delivered very strong results.
  • Cancellation rates improved this month, reducing to 12.33%, which is lower compared to previous months.

What Worked

  • Ruhi 7 Layer Necklace and Nita Ambani Necklace continued to be the best-selling products.
  • The older Ruhi 7 Layer creative initially declined in December, but after relaunching and testing it across multiple audiences, one interest-based audience picked up and delivered very strong performance.
  • Sehr Gold Brass Choker combo showed high traction in terms of both ROAS and sales.
  • A new product testing campaign launched mid-month delivered encouraging results with an overall ROAS of 1.95.
  • Broad women and new LAL audiences performed very well throughout the month.
  • An interest-based audience targeting Dance (Art) delivered strong results in terms of both ROAS and sales.

What Didn’t Work

  • The all-collection catalog sales campaign failed to gain traction and was paused.
  • Nita Ambani Necklace underperformed in December compared to its performance in previous months.
  • The previously best-performing campaign, CBO_3Aud_Broad_19Jul25, showed a consistent decline during the last 15 days of December despite multiple optimizations and was paused toward month end.
  • The Sehr collection–focused campaign did not deliver results at the same level as the previous month.

Next Month’s Strategy

Revenue Target

Maximizing returns on ad spend will remain the top priority while targeting a realistic and achievable revenue of ₹6,79,343 for the month.

ROAS Discipline

ROAS will be maintained at a minimum threshold of 1.82X. Campaigns will be closely monitored and scaled wherever there is potential to drive higher revenue efficiently.

Creative Strategy

We will develop and deploy more creatives inspired by top-performing formats, with a stronger focus on new shoot creatives that have been consistently delivering better performance.

Product Consistency

Increased focus will be placed on the Kundan and Chamak Dhamak collections to identify and scale additional winning products.

Scaling Flexibility

We will maintain flexibility to aggressively scale campaigns whenever existing campaigns demonstrate strong and sustainable performance signals.
