Meta Platform

Meta Deep Dive

Detailed narrative of how Meta campaigns performed across BAU and Testing in March 2026.

📈
BAU Campaigns — Strong Month Overall
In March, the BAU campaigns performed strongly, achieving ~2.07x ROAS (post cancellations) with a total spend of ₹3.17L. This month, we recorded the highest revenue to date at ₹7.31L, with additional upside from phone-based upsell conversions, further boosting overall revenue.
🚀
Week 2 Peak — 2.98x ROAS, Zero Cancellations
Performance peaked during the early phase of the month, particularly in Week 2, where ROAS reached 2.98x. With zero cancellations filed during this period, we capitalised on the momentum by scaling campaigns to maximise revenue.
🔗
Campaign Consolidation — Unified Structure
We consolidated all BAU campaigns into a single campaign, bringing together all top-performing creatives to streamline performance and optimisation. As part of this consolidation, campaigns including Sehr and Nita Ambani collections were merged into one unified structure.
✅
Cancellation Rates — All-Time Low
Cancellation rates remained stable, reaching an all-time low cancellation rate of 7.45%, indicating improved order quality throughout the month.
📉
Late-Month Decline — Weeks 4 & 5
However, performance declined in the latter part of the month, with BAU ROAS dropping to 1.35x in Week 4 and 1.38x in the final week of March. This decline was further impacted by near-zero purchases from testing campaigns, pulling overall monthly ROAS down from 2.1x+ to ~1.9x. A ~6% drop in AOV (vs January) also contributed to the drop, although improved CVR helped partially offset the impact.
🔍
Testing Campaigns — No Winners Identified
We increased focus on testing new products and creatives to identify future winners. However, none of the testing campaigns yielded a winning product. Overall testing performance closed at approximately 1.07x ROAS (post cancellations). A detailed breakdown by product is provided below.

Root Cause Analysis
📉
CPM Down 15% — Lower-Intent Audiences
CPM reduced by 15%, indicating delivery is shifting toward relatively lower-cost, potentially lower-intent audiences — a sign of audience quality degradation in the auction.
👁️
CTR Down 26% — Audience Quality Impact
CTR declined by 26%. With both CPM and CTR dropping simultaneously, this suggests a notable compound impact on audience quality — fewer relevant users are seeing and engaging with the ads.
⚙️
Budget Adjustments — Auction Repositioning
There haven't been any major structural changes, but recent budget adjustments may have influenced auction re-positioning — this was the only key change made to BAU in the past few days and likely triggered the shift in delivery.
🎯
Engaged vs. New Audience ROAS Divergence
ROAS from engaged audiences improved by 15%, while ROAS from new audiences declined by 40%. Mid-funnel metrics (LPV to ATC and ATC to COI) appear improved, but this is a downstream effect of weaker top-funnel traffic quality.
🛒
72% Drop in COI-to-Purchase — Checkout Trust Gap
A 72% drop in COI to purchase doesn't just point to lower-quality traffic — it may also indicate an issue at the final stage of checkout. While no concrete data confirms this, it suggests a potential trust gap among new users, likely driven by lower-quality traffic entering the funnel.
Next Steps & Optimisations Taken
Actions taken to stabilise and recover performance
01 Paused consistently low-performing ads, including some of the earlier winning ads that had fatigued.
02 Refreshed creatives by adding 3 new video assets to BAU to improve CTR and allowing the algorithm to explore a new audience set.
03 Reduced spends by around 50% to bring ROAS under control and stabilise performance.
04 Conducted a manual review of the website and checkout journey for any loading issues or glitches — no concerns were found.
05 Revalidated whether any previously visible checkout offers had been discontinued — no changes were identified.
06 Paused ongoing product testing campaigns due to lack of traction and shifted to running All Product Catalogue and Necklace Category campaigns instead.
07 Reallocated budgets from creative-heavy new product testing towards a more catalogue-focused approach.
08 Added 1% LAL of purchasers in the audience suggestion to help guide the campaign towards a more relevant audience at this stage.
